// THE PRIVACY PROMISE

Your AI. Your data.

Jessi is private by architecture, not by promise. Here's exactly what that means.

  1. 01
    Everyday chat and voice run on your phone. Your conversations are processed on-device, not on our servers.
  2. 02
    Your history, vault and memory stay local. What Jessi learns about you lives on your device, and only you can delete it.
  3. 03
    Never used for training. Jessi is trained on approved, sanitized material, never on your personal data.
  4. 04
    The cloud routes, it doesn't store. Sync between your devices passes through. It never parks there.
  5. 05
    No selling. No ad tracking. We don't sell your information or use it for targeted ads.
  6. 06
    Online only when you ask. News, sources, weather and connected services use the internet when you open them.
